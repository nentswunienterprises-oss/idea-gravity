THE IDEA GRAVITY CONSTITUTION
VERSION 1

PREAMBLE
Idea Gravity exists to discover, articulate, strengthen, and amplify meaningful ideas.
We believe that ideas shape people, people shape institutions, and institutions shape society.
Yet not all ideas possess equal force.
Some attract communities, resources, opportunities, and movements.
Others disappear despite attention, funding, and promotion.
The difference is gravity.
Idea Gravity exists to understand this phenomenon and increase the gravitational pull of ideas worthy of enduring influence.
We do not manufacture significance.
We reveal it.

ARTICLE I
THE NATURE OF GRAVITY
Gravity is the force that causes people, communities, resources, opportunities, institutions, and movements to organize around an idea.
Gravity cannot be purchased.
Gravity cannot be forced.
Gravity cannot be fabricated.
Gravity emerges when an idea possesses sufficient meaning, coherence, relevance, truth, and usefulness that people voluntarily orient themselves around it.

ARTICLE II
THE AMPLIFICATION PRINCIPLE
Amplification does not create significance.
Amplification reveals significance.
Attention can increase awareness.
Promotion can increase visibility.
Distribution can increase reach.
None of these create gravity.
If significance is absent, amplification accelerates disappearance.
If significance is present, amplification accelerates adoption.
Idea Gravity therefore amplifies only after gravity is identified.

ARTICLE III
THE DISCOVERY PRINCIPLE
Every institution begins with a discovery.
A discovery is not a product.
A discovery is not a service.
A discovery is not a business model.
A discovery is a truth observed within reality.
The responsibility of Idea Gravity is to identify discoveries capable of supporting institutions.
The first question is always:
What was discovered?

ARTICLE IV
THE GRAVITY TEST
Before an idea is amplified, it must be evaluated.
The Gravity Test exists to determine whether gravity is absent, emerging, or active.
The test evaluates:
Discovery
Clarity
Distinctiveness
Doctrine
Identity
Community Potential
Institutional Potential
Ideas without gravity shall not be amplified.
Ideas with latent gravity shall be articulated.
Ideas with active gravity shall be amplified.

ARTICLE V
THE REJECTION PRINCIPLE
Not every idea deserves amplification.
Visibility is not entitlement.
Attention is not entitlement.
Scale is not entitlement.
When gravity is absent, amplification becomes noise.
Idea Gravity rejects amplification where gravity has not been discovered.
Rejection protects the institution.
Rejection protects the audience.
Rejection protects reality.

ARTICLE VI
THE ARTICULATION PRINCIPLE
Many discoveries fail not because they lack gravity.
They fail because they lack articulation.
Articulation transforms discovery into understanding.
Idea Gravity develops:
Names
Language
Doctrine
Identity
Narrative
Community Architecture
The goal is not invention.
The goal is expression.

ARTICLE VII
THE INSTITUTION PRINCIPLE
Ideas become institutions when they become transferable.
A founder can carry an idea.
A team can support an idea.
An institution preserves an idea.
Idea Gravity exists to help discoveries transition from dependence on individuals to continuity through institutions.
The highest expression of gravity is institutionalization.

ARTICLE VIII
THE COMMUNITY PRINCIPLE
Gravity attracts people.
Communities emerge around shared meaning.
Strong communities are not built through incentives alone.
They are built through identity, belief, participation, and contribution.
The role of Idea Gravity is to create conditions where meaningful communities can form naturally.

ARTICLE IX
THE RESPONSIBILITY OF INFLUENCE
Influence is not neutral.
Ideas shape decisions.
Decisions shape lives.
Lives shape societies.
Idea Gravity therefore carries a responsibility to exercise influence with integrity.
The pursuit of attention without purpose is rejected.
The pursuit of influence without meaning is rejected.
The pursuit of scale without significance is rejected.

ARTICLE X
THE STANDARD
Idea Gravity does not optimize for virality.
Idea Gravity does not optimize for trends.
Idea Gravity does not optimize for appearances.
Idea Gravity optimizes for gravity.
The measure of success is not how many people see an idea.
The measure of success is how many people organize around it.

DECLARATION
We believe meaningful ideas deserve gravity.
We believe gravity precedes amplification.
We believe discoveries deserve articulation.
We believe institutions preserve discoveries.
We believe communities emerge around meaning.
We exist to help meaningful ideas become worthy of orbit.
This is the purpose of Idea Gravity.
