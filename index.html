Yet many remain forgettable. Their problem is not visibility. Their problem is gravity. When an idea lacks mass, amplification merely accelerates its disappearance.

The Idea Gravity Constitution

A public standard for influence with integrity: gravity precedes amplification, discoveries deserve articulation, and institutions preserve discoveries.

Article I

The Nature of Gravity

Gravity causes people, communities, resources, opportunities, institutions, and movements to organize around an idea.

Article II

The Amplification Principle

Amplification does not create significance. Amplification reveals significance.

Article III

The Discovery Principle

Every institution begins with a discovery: a truth observed within reality.

Article IV

The Gravity Test

Ideas are evaluated for discovery, clarity, distinctiveness, doctrine, identity, community, and institutional potential.

Article V

The Rejection Principle

When gravity is absent, amplification becomes noise. Rejection protects reality.

Article VI

The Articulation Principle

Articulation transforms discovery into understanding. The goal is expression.

Article VII

The Institution Principle

Ideas become institutions when they become transferable and endure beyond individuals.

Article VIII

The Community Principle

Strong communities are built through identity, belief, participation, and contribution.

Article IX

The Responsibility of Influence

The pursuit of attention without purpose and scale without significance is rejected.

Article X

The Standard

The measure of success is not how many people see an idea, but how many organize around it.

We exist to help meaningful ideas become worthy of orbit.
